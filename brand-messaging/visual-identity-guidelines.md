# Visual Identity Guidelines

## Overview

The **CyberShield Visual Identity** is designed to communicate intelligence, trust, and precision — reflecting the same calm control users experience inside the **CyberShield Control Hub**.

Every color, typeface, and layout choice reinforces the brand’s core personality: **smart, secure, and seamless.**

> 💡 **Tip:** A visual identity should extend the product experience — what users see in the interface should mirror what they feel in the content.

***

***

### Typography

CyberShield uses **modern sans-serif typography** for high readability across screens.\
The combination of clean headers and simple body fonts matches the clarity of its technical and marketing tone.

| **Font Family**          | **Usage**                        | **Style Example**                           |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Inter / Roboto**       | Primary UI & body text           | _Used across dashboards and content blocks_ |
| **Poppins / Montserrat** | Marketing headlines              | _Used in web pages and hero banners_        |
| **Roboto Mono**          | Code snippets and SDK references | _Used in developer documentation_           |

**Hierarchy Guidelines:**

* **H1 (Hero):** 36–48px — Bold, uppercase, clean
* **H2:** 28–32px — Semi-bold, sentence case
* **Body Text:** 16–18px — Regular weight
* **UI Labels:** 12–14px — Compact and legible

> 💡 **Tip:** Avoid over-styling. CyberShield’s typography works best when text feels _functional_, not decorative.

***

### Iconography & UI Elements

CyberShield icons follow a **flat, geometric style** with rounded edges — aligning with modern enterprise design systems.

| **Element Type**      | **Design Guideline**                      | **Example Usage**                 |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **Navigation Icons**  | Monoline, outlined, single color          | Sidebar (Home, Alerts, Reports)   |
| **Status Indicators** | Minimalist circles or shields             | Online / Offline / Warning states |
| **Charts & Metrics**  | Rounded edges, clean grid layout          | Control Hub Analytics             |
| **Buttons**           | Rounded corners (`4px` radius), bold CTAs | Primary actions like “Run Scan”   |

***

### ️ Imagery & Graphics

* **Style:** Clean, technical, abstract — no literal hacking imagery.
* **Motif:** Circuit patterns, data grids, glowing shields, or network connections.
* **Lighting:** Cool-toned gradients (blue → indigo) to reflect trust and intelligence.
* **Illustrations:** Vector-based; minimal human figures.

> 💬 **Do:** Use subtle motion lines or blue overlays to communicate speed.
>
> 🚫 **Don’t:** Use stock “cyber threat” photos (hooded figures, binary rain).

***

### Layout & Composition

CyberShield layouts use a **card-based structure** inspired by the Control Hub dashboard.\
Content is modular, spacious, and visually balanced to maintain calmness in data-heavy screens.

| **Area**             | **Layout Principle**                         | **Example**          |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| **Landing Pages**    | 2-column layout (text left, visuals right)   | Product intro pages  |
| **Feature Sections** | Horizontal icon blocks with white space      | Marketing collateral |
| **Dashboards**       | 3–4 card panels with consistent padding      | App & case studies   |
| **Email Templates**  | Narrow, center-aligned text with CTA buttons | Campaigns            |

> 💡 **Tip:** Keep the same visual rhythm across docs, site, and app — users should instantly _recognize_ they’re still within CyberShield’s world.

***

### Best Practice Guidelines

✅ Use consistent spacing (8px grid system) across UI and marketing visuals.\
✅ Maintain high contrast between text and backgrounds for accessibility (WCAG AA+).\
✅ Keep imagery futuristic but grounded — focus on _clarity_, not _drama_.\
✅ Avoid mixing warm and cool tones; CyberShield’s palette is _cool and calculated_.\
✅ Reuse dashboard UI cues (icons, cards, gradients) in web and ad visuals for cohesion.

> 🧠 **Info:** A cohesive visual system turns a product into a _brand experience_ — every design element reinforces trust and recognizability.

By following these principles, CyberShield delivers a cohesive brand experience across product, marketing, and design.
